Udaipur, Rajsthan


often called the "City of Lakes" or the "Venice of the East"—is a romantic destination filled with grand palaces, serene lakes, and stunning Mewar architecture.

  1. City Palace Explore Rajasthan's largest palace complex, featuring intricate mirrored walls, courtyards, and museum galleries.
  2. Jag Mandir Visit this exquisite 17th-century island palace situated right in the middle of Lake Pichola.
  3. Saheliyon-ki-Bari Stroll through the "Garden of the Maids," famous for its lush lawns, lotus pools, and marble fountains.
  4. Lake Pichola Take a sunset boat ride on this iconic, peaceful lake surrounded by hills and palaces.
  5. Mahal Take a boat ride on this iconic, peaceful lake surrounded by hills and palaces.
  • Dal Baati Churma: The ultimate Rajasthani comfort food featuring round, baked lentils served with hard wheat rolls dipped in pure ghee and sweet crumbled jaggery.
  • Ker Sangri: A traditional and unique berry-and-bean vegetable dish cooked with local spices.
  • Mewari Khad Murg: A rich, traditional hunter's dish where chicken is marinated in local spices and slow-cooked underground.
